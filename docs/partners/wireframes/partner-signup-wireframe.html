Partner signup — wireframe & component spec

One professional multi-step form for every partner type (host · venue · broker · sponsor · agency), with an AI co-pilot that reads & writes the form — partners can fill fields or just talk. Route /partners/signup?type=…. Ends in the partner dashboard. Full journeys + stakeholders: tasks/design/docs/partner-journeys.md.

audience: all partners (supply + B2B) stepper + form │ AI chat AI prefills · explains · acts EN only · Phase 2

The big idea

  • One typed wizard — ?type=host|venue|broker|sponsor|agency adapts the steps; far less to build than 5 flows.
  • AI co-pilot acts on the form (generative UI): "I run a rooftop bar in Provenza" → sets type=venue·category=nightclub·barrio=Provenza.
  • Autosave + resume between steps; verify (Google Business/socials/payout) gates "go live".
  • Same co-pilot continues in the dashboard ("draft a Friday post", "reply to this lead").
