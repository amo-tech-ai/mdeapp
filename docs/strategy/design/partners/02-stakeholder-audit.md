## Priority verdict (what to acquire first)

**P0 supply that compounds demand:** Event Hosts, Nightclubs, Real-estate hosts — they create the inventory Camila/Andrés/Tourist already want. **P1 revenue expansion:** Sponsors + Agencies (high $, low infra). **Defer:** marketplace vendors, hotels, tourism boards (Phase 3+).
