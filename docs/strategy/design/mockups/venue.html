About

Carmen is one of Medellín's defining fine-dining rooms — a quietly luxurious space in El Poblado where chefs Rob Pevitts and Carmen Angel cook a contemporary Colombian menu built on coastal seafood, Antioquian produce and a serious wine cellar.

Come for the multi-course tasting menu on the candlelit terrace, or order à la carte at the bar. Service is polished but unhurried, and the kitchen is happy to walk you through pairings. Book ahead for weekends — tables after 8 PM go fast.
