# Chunk 05 — Subscription Plans

> Goal: packaging that bills before liquidity (business side) and builds loyalty (consumer side). Subscriptions are the predictable-MRR backbone of the hybrid model.

## 5.1 Business-side tiers (the money tiers)

| Plan | Price | Key features | Limits | Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free / Claim** | $0 | Claimed listing, basic profile, capped leads/mo, reply to reviews | Capped leads, no automation, MDE branding | Long-tail supply, TOFU |
| **Pro** | **$49–$99/mo** | Featured eligibility, WhatsApp inbox, analytics, unlimited leads, 1 AI content channel | 1 location, limited campaigns | Cafés, single venues, solo agents |
| **Business** | **$199–$399/mo** | Pro + multi-channel AI marketing, booking automation, priority placement, review mgmt, 3 locations | Fair-use AI credits | Restaurants, tour ops, agencies |
| **Enterprise** | **$1,000–$8,000/mo** | Custom agents, API, white-label, dedicated success, SLA, multi-location, data feeds | Negotiated | Hotel groups, chains, Colombiamoda, brokerages |

## 5.2 Consumer-side (keep light — never gate discovery)

| Plan | Price | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Full AI concierge, search, maps, booking, tickets |
| **MDE+ (optional)** | **$5–$9/mo** | Priority concierge, exclusive event access, VIP perks, no booking fees, partner discounts |

> MDE+ is a *later* lever — a perks/loyalty play, not a paywall. Target expats/nomads who already pay for convenience.

## 5.4 Pricing psychology & packaging tactics

| Tactic | Why |
|---|---|
| **3 tiers + enterprise** | Decoy effect anchors Business as "the obvious choice" |
| **Annual prepay (2 mo free)** | Pulls cash forward, slashes churn |
| **Free tier = claim listing** | Top-of-funnel supply capture; upgrade path built in |
| **Usage-based AI credits (fair use)** | Protects COGS without nickel-and-diming |
| **Setup fee** | Filters tire-kickers, funds onboarding labor |
| **Local SMB pricing in COP** | Reduce FX sticker shock for local-only businesses |

## 5.5 Expansion-revenue levers (net revenue retention)

| Lever | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Tier upgrade | Pro → Business as ROI proves out |
| Add-on agents | Lead-qual / support agent on top of base |
| Multi-location | Per-location pricing for chains |
| Take-rate | Transactions stack on top of subscription |
| Paid placement | Featured/sponsored auctioned during high-demand windows |

> **Target NRR > 110%:** even with some logo churn, expansion within retained accounts should grow revenue. This is the metric that makes the financial model in Chunk 08 work.
