## 2.9 Revenue-line decision rule

> **Charge the side that makes money from the transaction.** Business/supply side pays (they monetize the booking); consumer side stays frictionless to build liquidity. The only consumer charge early on is an *optional* perks subscription (MDE+), never a gate on core discovery.
