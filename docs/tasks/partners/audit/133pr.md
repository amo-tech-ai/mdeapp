## 1. What this PR does (plain English + real-world)
**Before:** On `https://www.mdeai.co/partners`, 7 of the 8 funnel cards (Venues, Rentals, Sponsors, Business AI, Restaurants, Cafés, Nightlife) linked to landing pages that **don't exist → 404**. *Real-world:* a restaurant owner clicks "Start as restaurant" and hits a dead page — the funnel leaks the lead.

**After:** Every card routes to the **live** typed signup, e.g. a restaurant owner clicks → `/partners/signup?type=venue&category=restaurant` → the signup form opens **pre-filled with "Restaurant"** as the category. Zero dead links; the venue subtype is preserved end-to-end (and across the login round-trip).
