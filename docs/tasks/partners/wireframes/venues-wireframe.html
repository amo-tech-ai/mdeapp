For Venues — wireframe & component spec

B2B landing for restaurants, bars, cafés & event spaces (route /venues). Modeled on Mindtrip's /business/hotels ("Be the Hero of Every Stay"). Goal: get a venue to book a demo / list. Sells the services we offer venues: AI event booking · AI marketing (Postiz) · automated event ingestion (OpenClaw) · listing & visibility · leads · reporting.

audience: venue owners (supply) CTA = Book a demo / List your venue revenue: featured placement + AI services EN only · Phase 1/2

What we offer venues (the services this page sells)

  • AI event booking — guests request/book events; AI drafts replies + schedules (HITL approve)
  • AI marketing — Postiz — auto-write & schedule social posts for your events across channels
  • Automated event ingestion — OpenClaw — your events pulled into mdeai listings, no manual entry
  • Listing & visibility — appear in concierge answers + browse + map (grounded, verified)
  • Leads & CRM — capture inquiries; HITL approve/send AI replies
  • Reporting — views, leads, bookings analytics
