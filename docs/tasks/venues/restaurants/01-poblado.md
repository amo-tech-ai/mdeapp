## Booking workflow

The booking workflow should be honest and non-instant: user requests a reservation, AI drafts a WhatsApp or web message, human approval or user confirmation occurs, then the message is sent and the booking status is tracked. That matches your platform principle of propose-don’t-apply and avoids fake reservation promises.
